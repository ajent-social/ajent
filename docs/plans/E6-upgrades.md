# E6 -- Automated upgrades

Acceptance: Script installs update themselves automatically and verifiably. Package-managed installs are upgraded by their manager with clear guidance. Every upgrade leaves harness integrations pointing at a working binary. Decision: docs/adr/005-upgrade-strategy.md.
fidelity: executable

Parallel layout: T6.1 (detection) and T6.2 (release discovery, semver, state, fakerelease) have no dependency on each other. T6.3 owns `internal/update/apply.go`, the detach helper and `cmd/ajent/cmd_update.go`, including the shared `startDetached` and `autoRun` seams and the hidden `--auto-run` flag. T6.4, T6.5 and T6.6 attach through the `sessionStartExtras` hook slice (T4.2) and the `wire` slice (T2.6) from their own files, so none of them edits `cmd_hook.go` or `main.go`. Contracts: docs/tasks/T6.*.md.
